# Expanded Fronts — 2026-10-05

Implemented from the approved tank/map production plan: 12 playable tank families, 72 separately authored equal-footprint model sprites, 24 distinct environment/map choices, and 18 campaign operations. All tank families have three customized weapon slots and a rechargeable signature ability. Poison, cold, terrain drilling, knockback, ricochet, charged shots, map hardness, low gravity and warned lava hazards affect combat. Prediction, live projectiles and AI use the same weapon physics. Enemy special ammunition is bounded.

Collection costs start at 300 PT and rise by 75 PT per new family. Expedition wins also award new families. Every five wins with a tank grants 150 PT mastery credit. Appearance progression uses total part levels 1/6/12/18/25; importing old saves retains the former appearance stage, currency, parts and campaign records. Existing tanks remain owned. The hidden test code remains Ctrl+Shift+F8 → AFTERLIGHT980 (+10,000 local PT); mobile: tap the brand five times and enter the same code. It is not an online currency grant.

All new models are preserved as generated original RGBA source rows under authoring/growth-sprites, extracted into six independent runtime PNGs by tools/extract-expansion-sprites.py. New Blender scenes and their geometry generation are under authoring/environments and tools/build-expansion-environments.py. Runtime GLBs pack authored textures and batch by material. Environments are visual scenery; gameplay collision surfaces are the explicit, destructible slabs drawn by the game. Scenery structures do not create invisible collision walls.

Validation: 36 unit/integration tests, including victories in all 18 missions with affordable progression; all 36 tank weapon combinations; 24 unique GLBs and 72 unique sprite hashes; mirrored collision surfaces and surviving lower floors; finite status effects; legacy save migration, collection and mastery. Browser checks exercise all 24 maps and 12 tanks, mobile 390x844 and 844x390, actual victory/rewards/reload, recoil/hit/ground feedback, movement sound/mute, and 153 cached offline assets. Official develop-web-game client also exercised movement and fire and captured the game canvas and text state. Browser reports are saved under research/. Old weapon regression checks all 36 legacy weapons and existing transport delegation.

Existing online play remains accessible through /original.html. New families/maps/progression are campaign and AI content. Production rooms API availability is verified separately; human multiplayer, cross-device WebRTC matches and server-authoritative new content are not claimed validated. Human PvP balance targets require later playtesting; passing deterministic AI scenarios is not proof of 45–55% matchup balance.

Production deployment uses tools/deploy.sh against the verified static root, with full tar backup and SHA-256 validation. Restore is tested into an isolated folder before reporting success. API, database, proxy and backend processes are preserved.
